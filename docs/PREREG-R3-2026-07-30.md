## 1. Purpose and exact claim scope

R3 asks whether WeVibe's recall-pivot policy retires planted harmful memories when real outcomes show they should not persist.

**Primary endpoint — exactly one:** paired within-run policy contrast for PLANTED BAD ARCHETYPES. Standing is a pure function of (events, policy) and replay is proven bit-exact, so ONE bench event log is replayed under TWO policies: (a) `edge-policy-v1` AS SHIPPED, and (b) the same policy with E3 outcome events IGNORED. Survival of the SAME planted bads is compared under each. This is the spec's `noOut` baseline column computed on REAL evidence instead of simulated.

Every planted bad is its own control, so the design tests an ~0.80-vs-0.12 paired difference rather than 0.12-vs-0.20 against binomial noise. Unit of analysis: ARCHETYPE (K=14), with instance-level (K=42) as SECONDARY.

Test: McNemar exact conditional binomial on the DISCORDANT pairs, ONE-SIDED in the pre-registered direction. Survival is a paired BINARY outcome. Pre-registered DIRECTION: bads survive under the outcome-ignoring policy and die under the shipped policy (b), with ~zero the reverse (c).

Pre-registered MINIMUM EFFECT SIZE: `min_risk_difference = 0.50` and `max_reverse_discordant = 1`, at alpha 0.05. This is the pre-commitment; without it the endpoint is still a forking path. Basis for 0.50: it is the LOWER edge of the sim's own observed paired diffs vs the noOut baseline (-0.50...-0.75), so it does not cherry-pick the favourable end. Basis for the reverse cap: at K=14, b=8/c=1 gives one-sided exact p≈0.0195, so the rule is satisfiable at the sample size we can afford.

Critical arithmetic: at K=14, `risk_difference = (b - c)/14`, so the 0.50 floor requires **b - c >= 7**. Verified: b=7,c=0 -> rd 0.500, p=0.0078 PASSES; b=8,c=1 -> rd 0.500, p=0.0195 PASSES; b=6,c=0 -> rd 0.4286, p=0.0156 FAILS the effect floor despite being significant. The MINIMUM EFFECT, not significance, is the binding constraint. That is the intent.

Decision rule = the CONJUNCTION of four pre-registered conditions, all of which must hold: direction (b>c), effect (rd >= 0.50), reverse cap (c <= 1), significance (p < 0.05).

Cost: ZERO additional cells — two policy replays over one event log. Implemented in `wevibe_bench/stats.py` as `mcnemar_exact`, `paired_binary_contrast`, `meets_minimum_effect`, with constants `PREREG_MIN_RISK_DIFFERENCE = 0.50` and `PREREG_MAX_REVERSE_DISCORDANT = 1`. Run manifest freezes the two arms as lever `L15_PAIRED_CONTRAST_ARMS = "shipped:edge-policy-v1|counterfactual:edge-policy-v1-outcomes-ignored"`.

Mandatory disclosure: the replay contrast is OFF-POLICY for at least one arm. Under the outcome-ignoring policy, DIFFERENT memories would actually have been served. Bias direction is CONSERVATIVE: without outcomes, bads survive longer, get served more, and generate more evidence, so the true gap would if anything be WIDER than the replay shows.

**Secondary absolute endpoint:** badPersist for PLANTED BAD ARCHETYPES, archetype-level, K=14 archetypes. Report as an exact Clopper-Pearson estimate with a 90% interval. The 0.20 line is a DESCRIPTIVE REFERENCE LINE, explicitly NOT a gate. Both endpoints are needed: if both policies leave badPersist at 0.45, the contrast is significant and the system still does not work. The contrast proves the MECHANISM; the absolute level proves the PRODUCT.

Everything else is **SECONDARY and DESCRIPTIVE**: lift (ON vs bmOFF), sham degradation, goodSurv, gfa, ttaBad, theta, rho, duplicate rate, contention covariates, token overhead, same-task lift, held-out-variant lift, and the instance-level paired contrast.

The manifest must freeze this primary endpoint next to the policy hash before any numbers exist.

Headline order:

1. Primary: paired within-run policy contrast for outcome-driven retirement.
2. Secondary/product: absolute badPersist and T1/T6 outcome-driven retirement.
3. Secondary/supporting: lift.

Same-task and held-out-variant lift are scored separately. The same-task number is never a headline. The claim scope is durable compiled solutions / memory-trust behavior, not broad capability lift.

Pre-registered possible outcome: planted bads are actively harmful, not inert. A corpus with surviving bads can push ON below OFF. If ON falls below OFF, that is a genuine result, not a failed benchmark.

## 9. Success / failure framing

R3 does not turn on a single pass/fail gate. The primary endpoint is the paired within-run policy contrast at the planted-bad archetype level. The secondary absolute badPersist endpoint is an exact Clopper-Pearson estimate with a 90% interval. The T1 0.20 bar is a descriptive reference line, not a gate.

Success-shaped result: planted bad archetypes survive under the outcome-ignoring replay and die under the shipped replay with the pre-registered direction, minimum risk difference, reverse cap, and significance all satisfied; absolute badPersist is also low enough to make the product credible; good memories survive; and duplicate/harvest artifacts do not explain the outcome.

Failure-shaped result: bad archetypes persist, good memories over-retire, rho/harvest coverage is too broken to trust the denominator, or ON underperforms OFF because surviving bads actively harm the run.

An ON-below-OFF result is a real result if planted bads explain it. It is not a benchmark failure.

Lift is supporting evidence only. Same-task lift is never a headline.

## Limitations

1. **Test-mode retrieval does not exercise the production governor.** Bench runs floor 0 / budget 1000; production serves 3 at floor 0.55 — roughly 300x the serve breadth. Our own sim says the retrieval regime is the DOMINANT variable: the query-regime sweep moved both models by ~0.19 while policies separated by only 0.01-0.05, and at top-K=1 both models breached constraints outright. These results characterise the standing mechanism under test-mode retrieval and have NOT been shown to transfer to the production governor. The reason is that the bench cannot exercise the production governor. This is not the same fact as "the tension is deferred"; only the first belongs in limitations. An auto-approve-with-production-governor transfer-check cell is being built in parallel, and this sentence stands as the fallback if that cell is not run.
2. **The sim can no longer serve as independent evidence for the policy it selected.** The bench is now validating the simulator as much as the policy. Say it out loud.
3. **A window creates selection pressure toward fast-resolving knowledge.** Architectural insight that takes three days to validate never gets credited, and no credit plus idle decay equals retirement. The corpus will systematically prefer quick fixes. This is a DISCLOSED DESIGN CONSEQUENCE. Hedge: window expiry should emit an explicit unresolved-within-window observation so a future policy can distinguish "never seen" from "seen but slow". Implementation is owned elsewhere, not by this document.
4. **CCN-vs-IDN scope limit on theta.** Theta is scoped to the benchmark's observable canonical-correctness and identity-noise distinction; it is not a universal memory-quality measure.
5. **Theta per rung is NEVER pooled.** Report each rung's theta separately because model behavior changes the observation process.
6. **Archetype stratification remains binding.** Planted bads are headlined at archetype level, with instance-level analysis secondary.
7. **Retirement is headlined over lift.** Lift supports the claim but does not define it.
8. **T2/T3/T5 breach under the shipped policy.** This is an OPEN TARGET CONFLICT escalated to Walter. The max goodSurv observed anywhere in the sweep is 0.8162 (at rho=1.0), so T2 >=0.90 is UNREACHABLE AT ANY rho under held credit. No new T2 number is proposed here; that is Walter's call.
