## 1. Purpose and exact claim scope

R3 asks whether WeVibe's recall-pivot policy retires planted harmful memories when real outcomes show they should not persist.

**Primary endpoint — exactly one:** badPersist for PLANTED BAD ARCHETYPES, archetype-level, K=14 archetypes. Report as an exact Clopper-Pearson estimate-with-interval, with the one-sided upper bound alongside. Evaluate against the T1 bar of 0.20 as a DESCRIPTIVE comparison, not a pass/fail gate.

Everything else is **SECONDARY and DESCRIPTIVE**: lift (ON vs bmOFF), sham degradation, goodSurv, gfa, ttaBad, theta, rho, duplicate rate, contention covariates, token overhead, same-task lift, and held-out-variant lift.

The manifest must freeze this primary endpoint next to the policy hash before any numbers exist.

Headline order:

1. Primary: T1/T6 outcome-driven retirement.
2. Secondary/supporting: lift.

Same-task and held-out-variant lift are scored separately. The same-task number is never a headline. The claim scope is durable compiled solutions / memory-trust behavior, not broad capability lift.

Pre-registered possible outcome: planted bads are actively harmful, not inert. A corpus with surviving bads can push ON below OFF. If ON falls below OFF, that is a genuine result, not a failed benchmark.

### 2c. Fixtures

### 2d. Seed / pairing, N, and flakiness screen

Paired cells use the same fixture, seed/scaffold, and run scaffold. Arms are pair members. Pairs are blocked adjacently in time so provider drift hits pair members equally.

Main recommendation: **N=8 paired per cell**. Hard floor: **N=6 paired per cell**. N=6 is the arithmetic floor at which a two-sided paired sign test can reach p<0.05 at all (`2/2^6 = 0.031`). Below 6, p-values are misleading regardless of effect size.

Flakiness screen comes first:

- hy3: 5 OFF-only repeats x 3 fixtures = 15 cells.
- k2.7-code: 3 OFF-only repeats x 3 fixtures = 9 cells.

Classify each fixture/rung as always-pass, always-fail, or flaky. Deterministic fixtures have zero power to show lift; concentrate main N on the flaky band.

### 2e. Planted bads

Plant 14 archetypes x 3 instances = K=42. Archetype is the headline unit. Instance-level analysis is secondary. Report intra-archetype correlation (ICC) to justify pooling.

## 9. Success / failure framing

R3 does not turn on a single pass/fail gate. The primary endpoint is an estimate-with-interval for badPersist at the planted-bad archetype level. The T1 0.20 bar is a descriptive comparison.

Success-shaped result: planted bad archetypes retire quickly enough that badPersist is low with an interval that makes the policy look credible, while good memories survive and duplicate/harvest artifacts do not explain the outcome.

Failure-shaped result: bad archetypes persist, good memories over-retire, rho/harvest coverage is too broken to trust the denominator, or ON underperforms OFF because surviving bads actively harm the run.

An ON-below-OFF result is a real result if planted bads explain it. It is not a benchmark failure.

Lift is supporting evidence only. Same-task lift is never a headline.
